About InterVia

One team, every stage of the project.

InterVia Decoration is a full-service interior design, architecture, and fit-out firm headquartered in Doha, with a distributed team working across Qatar, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, the UK, and the US.

We take projects from first sketch through construction and final handover — design, engineering, procurement, and site execution under one roof, so clients manage one relationship instead of five contractors.

What we do

Services across the full build lifecycle.

Full-spectrum engineering, design, and delivery services — from first concept through construction and handover.

01 — Design

Architecture Design

Concept development, schematic design, and construction documentation for residential, commercial, and institutional projects.

02 — Design

Interior Design

Space planning, material specification, and finish selection for residential, commercial, and hospitality interiors.

03 — Planning

Urban Planning

Master planning, land-use strategy, and site layout for large-scale developments and communities.

01 — Engineering

Structural Analysis and Design

Structural calculations, member sizing, and detailed drawings for reinforced concrete and steel structures.

02 — Engineering

Value Engineering & Feasibility

Cost optimization and technical analysis to maximize project value without compromising quality or performance.

03 — Infrastructure

Road and Pavement Design

Horizontal and vertical alignment, pavement structure design, and drainage for roads and access networks.

04 — Engineering

Engineering Design & Innovation

Cross-disciplinary engineering integration and creative problem-solving for complex project requirements.

01 — Build

Fit-Out Works and Execution

On-site delivery of interior fit-out from partitioning and ceilings to joinery, finishes, and handover.

02 — Documentation

Shop Drawing Production

Detailed fabrication and installation drawings coordinated with structural, architectural, and MEP disciplines.

03 — Manage

Project Planning & Design Management

Programme development, design coordination, and milestone tracking from concept through construction.

01 — Technology

Building Information Modeling

3D modeling, clash detection, and LOD 300–500 deliverables across all project phases.
